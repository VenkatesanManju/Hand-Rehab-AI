Understanding Range of Motion (ROM)

Clear definition and details of your joint movement measurements

Movement Guide
💡
ROM Definition: ROM measures how far a joint can move, in degrees.
🖐️
Specific Joint Measured

Index & Middle Finger Knuckles (Proximal Interphalangeal - PIP joints).

🔄
Specific Movement Measured

Active finger flexion: curling fingers inward from a straight open palm to a tight closed fist.

📐
Measurement Scale

0° indicates a flat open palm (full extension); 100° indicates a complete closed fist (maximum active flexion).

🔍
Actual Measurement vs. AI Forecast

Your recorded ROM (e.g. 100°) is the single highest peak flexion achieved during camera exercise today. The AI forecast (e.g. 68.5°) is a smoothed 14-day rolling trend projection estimating your sustained average movement capability.
